## 9. First three campaigns

**1. c/0015** (GO, 2 reachable, total 25)
- entry rung: $\Qd$ uniform over single keywords, where $\SLC_{\Qd}(T) = \frac{1}{m}\sum_{u \in I(T)} \HW(\bar u)$: is best-first within an absolute constant of the minimum total Hamming weight of internal labels?
- plan class: c_refutation
- milestone: Exhaustive search at $n \le 10$, $m \le 6$ over the single-keyword case: either an instance with $\SLC/\Opt \ge 3$, which already rules out the small constants and tells the Prover what shape to expect, or an exact certificate that the ratio never exceeds a specific rational at those parameters, which is the base case a charging argument would be built on. Provable or refutable in under two pages.
- yield on failure: Either outcome is a result. A growing ratio in $n$ refutes the conjecture and settles the paper's question; a flat ratio pins the constant to aim at and identifies the worst-case instance family the charging argument has to be tight on.
- human input needed first: whether a bound for one canonical tie-breaking rule counts as an answer or only as a partial one, whether the single-keyword case alone is an acceptable freeze, which the page's own remark says the drafter would regard as the real content

**2. c/0034** (GO, 1 routine, total 26)
- entry rung: Round two of the stripping recursion: given a parity oracle guaranteed only on $\psi_2^{d}$, obtain the second parity soundly, i.e. tolerate exactly one halving of the error
- plan class: a_reduction
- milestone: At $d = 6$ and $q \in \{7,11,13\}$, compute exactly the statistical distance between $\psi_2^{d}$ and the round-two error distribution, and decide whether adding fresh noise can bridge it without destroying the parity just stripped. Either a smudging step with a polynomial loss, or a proof that any re-widening randomises the recovered parity, which classifies the obstruction as ESSENTIAL. Under two pages either way.
- yield on failure: If re-widening provably destroys the stripped parity, that is a clean negative result about the source's own heuristic and tells the NTRU instantiation line that its scheme's one-wayness rests on a genuinely weaker assumption than Ring-LWE. That is worth publishing and is more informative than the affirmative answer.
- human input needed first: explicit confirmation that the on-distribution clause is binding, since dodging it is the highest-scoring wrong answer, whether a reduction from the first-is-errorless decision variant counts as an answer, given that the source's second heuristic already gives one

**3. c/0036** (PROBE, 3 stretch, total 19)
- entry rung: $|D| \in \{4,8\}$, two queries: does there exist a unitary flipping operator for which Unruh's compressed permutation oracle is distinguishable from a random permutation with inverse access?
- plan class: c_refutation
- milestone: Enumerate or optimise over flipping operators at $|D| = 4$ and compute the exact two-query distinguishing advantage against the ideal object. Either a counterexample, which refutes the conjecture and explains why Carolan's injectivity maintenance is necessary, or a certificate that no flipping operator distinguishes at that size, which is evidence for soundness and identifies the invariant. Under two pages.
- yield on failure: Either result is publishable and short. A counterexample settles the page; a certificate at small $|D|$ hands the Prover the invariant to generalise. The one outcome to avoid is spending a Prover wave before this check runs.
- human input needed first: explicit confirmation that the target is Unruh's construction with an ARBITRARY flipping operator, and that Carolan's theorem does not settle it, since this is contested and is the whole content, whether the payoff still justifies the campaign at all, now that the research question this conjecture served has been answered by other means
