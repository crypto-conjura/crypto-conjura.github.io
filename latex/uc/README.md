# UC encyclopedia source material

One document: `uc-functionalities-standalone.tex`, an encyclopedia of UC ideal
functionalities ordered by dependency depth, self-contained and needing no
`.bib` (its bibliography is an inlined `thebibliography` of 130 entries). It is
the survey the site's nine layers and their 104 entries were laid out from.

Each `\bibitem` carries a trailing comment recording how its bibliographic data
was checked -- `status:verified`, `status:cross-checked`, `status:from-canon`,
`status:from-list2`, `status:UNVERIFIED`. Eleven are still `UNVERIFIED`; grep
for it. Compile with two `pdflatex` passes, since the first writes the citation
labels and `longtable` column widths the second consumes.

## There is no drafts folder here any more

Until 18 August 2026 this folder also held 100 per-functionality skeletons,
`latex/uc/layer-N-.../<slug>/main.tex`, and both READMEs described writing an
entry into one and then regenerating its site page from it. That never
happened once: the folder had a single commit in its history, its skeletons
never gained a line of content, and by the time they were removed 42 entries
had been written without them. They are in git if they are ever wanted back.

**A UC entry is written directly to its site page.** `prompts/source.md` is the
per-entry prompt and `CONTRIBUTING.md` has the mechanics; in outline,
`scripts/uc_source.py` harvests the cited papers, the prose goes into
`uc/layer-N-.../<slug>/index.qmd`, and the interface box is generated by
`scripts/gen_interface.py` from a LaTeX fragment in
`surveys/uc-for-gamers/latex/functionalities/<slug>.tex`.

That fragment is the per-entry LaTeX artifact, and it is a better one than a
draft here could be: it is the actual source of what the page shows, and
`gen_interface.py --check` runs in both workflows and fails when a fragment and
its page disagree. A draft in this folder could never be gated that way, which
is how the skeletons were able to rot unnoticed for as long as they did.
