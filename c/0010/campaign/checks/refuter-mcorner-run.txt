==============================================================================
S3  MECHANISM CATALOGUE AT REALISTIC PARAMETERS
==============================================================================
Three mechanisms exhaust everything the search found.  Exact closed forms,
each checked against an independent Monte-Carlo simulation.

(a) HIT.  On an f-event of probability p both sources output one cell c*
    (E[m_i] = p + (1-p)/N <= delta), else uniform on [N].  Observer queries
    c* and accepts iff y = f(c*).   adv = p (1 - 1/M)  exactly.
    q queries -> q planted cells -> adv = min(1, q p)(1 - 1/M).
    M-dependence: the factor (1 - 1/M), bounded by 1.  Ceiling: q delta.

(b) PLANT.  Sources choose a KxK rectangle B1xB2 = argmax of the empirical
    density of an observer-side test set; observer runs the test.  The test
    set has density alpha in [M] BY CONSTRUCTION (e.g. accept iff
    f(u(y)) in S with |S| = alpha M), so the cell labels are i.i.d.
    Bernoulli(alpha) whatever M is: the mechanism is M-FREE identically.
    Measured below: the achievable bias a(N,K) and its independence of M.

(c) FIT.  Source flat on a KxK rectangle; observer spends q queries inside
    it and Bayes-tests the challenge against the partial histogram.
    EXACT:  adv = Phi(min(q,K^2), M) / K^2,  Phi(q,M) = M E[(Bin(q,1/M)-q/M)^+].
    Phi ~ min(q, sqrt(qM/2pi)): the M-dependence switches OFF for M >~ q.

==============================================================================
S3F  THE NEAR-MISS: A 1-QUERY CERTIFICATE FOR THE CHALLENGE, AND WHY ITS

==============================================================================
The only way a 1-query observer can certify that the challenge lies in a
planted set is to reproduce the challenge value at a challenge-determined
cell: fix u : [M] -> [N]^2 and accept iff f(u(y)) = y.  In Real_0 this
accepts with probability ~1/M.  In Real it accepts with probability 1 if
the source's rectangle consists of cells c with f(u(f(c))) = f(c).

==============================================================================
S4  CORNER AUDIT OVER A LARGE PARAMETER GRID
==============================================================================
For every grid point the best attack in the catalogue is compared with
  mfree = 5 sqrt(sigma' delta) + mu'(q)      (the conjectured M-free bound)
  D''   = 5 sqrt(sigma' delta) + min(mu'(min(qM,N^2)), 2 delta sqrt M)

VACUITY FRONTIER -- why the exact brute force cannot be run in the corner.
A corner point says anything only if the bound it replaces is below 1:
  5 sqrt(sigma' delta) < 1  and  2 delta sqrt M < 1, with delta >= 1/N.
The second gives M < 1/(4 delta^2) <= N^2/4, so the MEANINGFUL corner is
  sigma' q+/(4 delta) < M < 1/(4 delta^2),  non-empty iff sigma' q+ delta < 1.
The first forces delta < 1/(25 sigma') and hence N > 50 log2 N:
  sigma= 0 : smallest N with a non-vacuous point at delta=1/N is 2^9 = 512, i.e. |domain| = N^2 = 2.62e+05 cells and |Fun| = M^(N^2)
  sigma= 1 : smallest N with a non-vacuous point at delta=1/N is 2^9 = 512, i.e. |domain| = N^2 = 2.62e+05 cells and |Fun| = M^(N^2)

S6  WHAT THE OBSTRUCTION SUGGESTS: A TRANSCRIPT UNION IN PLACE OF THE
    UNION OVER ALL 2^M TESTS  (a lead for the prover, checked numerically)
==============================================================================
Where delta sqrt(M) comes from: Lemma B of kappa-2-r3 makes the rectangle
discrepancy bound hold simultaneously for ALL theta : [M] -> {0,1}, at a
cost of M ln 2 inside the numerator of t, which surfaces as delta sqrt(M).
That quantifier grants the observer a test depending on ALL of f.  A
q-query observer's test on challenge v depends on f only through the q
cells of THAT challenge's transcript, and (S1/S2/S3, exactly) the whole
signal is then the mass of the inspected cells:
      E[ rho(v) | transcript ] - 1/M = (n_v - q/M)/K^2,
      sum_v | . | = Phi(q,M)/K^2 <= q delta^2   for q <= M.
So the natural repair is to union over TRANSCRIPTS, not over tests:
      C_1 = M ln 2 + ln(4N^2/gamma_0)   ~~>   C_1' = q ln(N^2 M) + ln(4N^2/gamma_0),
giving  delta sqrt(2 C_1')  ~  delta sqrt(2 q ln(N^2 M))  in place of
delta sqrt(2 M ln 2).  Below: would that suffice for the Contract's target
      C sqrt(sigma' q+ delta) ?

  ratio R := [1.3013 sqrt(sigma' d) + d sqrt(2 q ln(N^2 M) + 2 ln(4N^2/d)) + d]
             / sqrt(sigma' q+ d)
  (numerator = Corollary D' with C_1 replaced by C_1'; a ratio bounded by
   an absolute constant means the repair reaches the Contract's bound.)

  CAVEAT.  This is an arithmetic feasibility check of a PROOF SKETCH, not
