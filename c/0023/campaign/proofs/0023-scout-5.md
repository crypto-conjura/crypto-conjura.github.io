## JOB 2 — EXTENSION: both targets found, both PROVED in print.

**(a) The inequality S7 recorded as (5) is Theorem 3.2, p. 6** (proof p. 6;
previewed unnumbered as (5) on p. 3). Verbatim in [S7b]. Hypotheses: $X=X_1
\times\cdots\times X_n$ a **finite** product set with no algebraic or measure
structure and unequal sizes allowed; $\mathbb F$ **any** field; $f$ non-zero;
**no** hypothesis on char $\mathbb F$ or on $|X_i|$ being invertible — I
looked for one specifically and there is none in §3. The conclusion is the
strong surjectivity form $\pi_{S^c}(\mathrm{supp}f)=X_{S^c}$, with the
numerical $\dim_\pi+\deg_\otimes\ge n$ as a corollary of the printed
definition $\dim_\pi(\mathcal A)=\max\{|T|:\pi_T(\mathcal A)=X_T\}$.

The one place a false transfer could hide — *what $\deg_\otimes$ means* — is
closed in print, twice. $\deg_\otimes(f)=\max\{|S|:f^{=S}\ne0\}$ for the
Efron–Stein decomposition relative to **chosen** complements $\mathbb F^{X_i}
=\langle1\rangle\oplus W_i$; the paper never asserts choice-independence, but
it does print the choice-free equivalent "*$\deg_\otimes(f)\le r$ means that
$f$ lies in the linear span of all functions depending on at most $r$
coordinates*" (p. 3). A prover quoting the general-field form should quote
that sentence alongside it.

**(b) The group version exists, is PROVED (not announced, not deferred), and
is exactly the parent Contract's setting.** §3.4, p. 7: **Corollary 3.4** —
$G=G_1\times\cdots\times G_n$ a product of **finite abelian** groups,
$f:G\to\mathbb C$ non-zero, then $\dim_\pi(\mathrm{supp}f)+\deg_{\hat G}(f)\ge
n$, where the paper *prints* $\deg(\chi):=|\{i:\chi_i\text{ non-trivial}\}|$
and $\deg_{\hat G}(f):=\max\{\deg(\chi):\hat f(\chi)\ne0\}$. That is the
Contract's degree **symbol for symbol**, and the four-line proof supplies the
identification $\deg_\otimes=\deg_{\hat G}$ itself (take $W_i$ = span of the
non-trivial characters of $G_i$). No restriction on the groups: not cyclic,
not prime order, not a $p$-group; sizes may differ per coordinate.

So, in Contract language: **for every finite abelian $\mathcal Y$, every $N$,
and every non-zero $f:\mathcal Y^N\to\mathbb C$ with $\deg(f)\le d$, there is
$W\subseteq[N]$ with $|W|\le d$ such that $\mathrm{supp}(f)$ projects ONTO
$\mathcal Y^{[N]\setminus W}$.** This resolves §E possibility (i)
affirmatively: the S7 mechanism is group-uniform with a $d$-sized loss, so it
is reusable at R2, at R3 (no two-valuedness needed), and at the **top rung**
for the existentially quantified group — with no scout inference in the chain.
Consequence: S7's flagged $\{0,1\}\to\{\pm1\}$ affine translation remark is no
longer load-bearing (Corollary 3.4 with $G_i=\mathbb Z_2$ is native).

## A caveat the Strategist should carry

**[SCOUT'S OWN REMARK, not printed.]** The *counting* consequence of Corollary
3.4 is only $|\mathrm{supp}f|\ge|\mathcal Y|^{N-d}$, i.e. density $\ge
|\mathcal Y|^{-d}$ — precisely card S1's K1 scale, for every group. So any
argument that uses this mechanism *only* through the density it implies is

the two it uses. S7's two obstacles to the R2 lead — pattern-dependence of the
conflicting trace, and non-canonicity of the window across a family — are
